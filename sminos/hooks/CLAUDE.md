# sminos/hooks/ — the sminos plugin's hooks

A path here is named from `sminos/` when it is the plugin's own file; `docs/`, `sminos/…` and `to-human/…` paths are the repository's. The function hooks' detail is in `hooks/mods/CLAUDE.md`.

The shell hooks: `hooks/smns-record.sh` on `Stop` and `SessionStart` passes the payload to `sminos record`, which records the session's config dir when its engine runs under a non-default one (the hook alone runs in the session's environment: `skills/sminos/scripts/CLAUDE.md`), joins the session to its project and exports what it sent its human since the last export (its `<message>` spans, code-aware, its accepted calls of a message tool — `mcp__to-human__message` or MAWS's `mcp__maws__message` (`MESSAGE_TOOLS`), each a `message` event at the call's place whose `src` is the tool-use id, an unanswered call holding the export's position — and its async asks and their answers) into the project's log, an answer to a question another session of the machine asked going under that session's writer (a copy answering its original's), and none of what a copy copied; it is bounded (5 s, `SMNS_RECORD_TIMEOUT`), exits 0 on any failure, and does nothing outside a project or in sminos's own internal forks (`SMNS_INTERNAL`).

`hooks/smns-derive.sh` on `SessionStart` with the `compact` source starts `sminos derive <session> --compacted` detached (`nohup`, its output appended to `daemon.log` as the daemon's `derive --all` is, since a failed fork's reason is said only on stderr) and exits 0 at once, printing nothing, so every third compaction since the session's last derived line (`DERIVE_COMPACTIONS`, `compaction_due`) derives its in-charge line and charge name again rather than leaving them to the daemon's six-hour spacing, the others only counted; `--compacted` waives that spacing and the no-activity rule (a set line, a brief's or an operator's, never refuses any derive: it stands until the next derived line), and clears the session's `now:` status line (`derive_refusal`, `clear_status`; design `docs/doperpowers/specs/2026-11-08-sminos-derive-on-compact-design.md`). It runs in the session's environment, as a Bash tool's `sminos derive` does; nothing in an internal fork or for a payload whose `session_id` is not a UUID.

`hooks/smns-cleared.sh` on `SessionEnd` keeps the payload of a `/clear` (reason `clear`: the session the process is leaving) as `$SMINOS_HOME/cleared/<CLAUDE_PID>.json`, by one write and a rename and with no Python, since an ending session's hooks are given little time; nothing for another reason or in an internal fork, and never an error. The new session's record hook and guide hook, in the same process, read it as the witness of the session left (`cleared_from`: a start of source `clear`, a note younger than a minute, written after the process started, naming another session), and the record hook moves the alias to the new session (T1's M3); the daemon's sweep removes a note once its process is gone.

`hooks/smns-guide.sh` on `SessionStart` (every source, compaction included) prints `sminos guide`'s text on a machine enrolled at a desk hub (`daemon/identity.json`, written at its endpoint's first welcome): the session's alias, short, project and machine, where that project comes from (its launch directory, its repository's remote, the machine's own project, or its `join`), that it is a thread (a lane A member; not said to a session MAWS launched), a line naming the channels its thread is in when it is in any (`sminos_channels.channels_of`; with where a reply line sends an answer: a member's frame's into the channel, a frame from a session not in it to that session's own project), and the verbs that find and reach the org's other sessions, speaking in a channel and, for a thread alone (`channel create` and `add` refuse a caller that is none), making one among them (CH2's M10, evaluated: without the channel line a session told to keep sessions of three repositories in one place asked them to `join` its project), and after a compaction or a resume the roster of the members it exchanged sminos messages with, read from the logs (where each is now, its state and in-charge line, the tag that reaches it, its last exchange, and whether that is unanswered); nothing on an unenrolled machine or in an internal fork, and never an error (design §9 of the org directory spec).
