# sminos/hooks/ — the sminos plugin's hooks

A path here is named from `sminos/` when it is the plugin's own file; `docs/`, `sminos/…` and `to-human/…` paths are the repository's. The function hooks' detail is in `hooks/mods/CLAUDE.md`.

The shell hooks: `hooks/smns-record.sh` on `Stop` and `SessionStart` passes the payload to `sminos record`, which records the session's config dir when its engine runs under a non-default one (the hook alone runs in the session's environment: `skills/sminos/scripts/CLAUDE.md`), joins the session to its project and exports what it sent its human since the last export (its `<message>` spans, code-aware, its accepted calls of a message tool — `mcp__to-human__message` or MAWS's `mcp__maws__message` (`MESSAGE_TOOLS`), each a `message` event at the call's place whose `src` is the tool-use id, an unanswered call holding the export's position — and its async asks and their answers) into the project's log, an answer to a question another session of the machine asked going under that session's writer (a copy answering its original's), and none of what a copy copied; it is bounded (5 s, `SMNS_RECORD_TIMEOUT`), exits 0 on any failure, and does nothing outside a project or in sminos's own internal forks (`SMNS_INTERNAL`).

`hooks/smns-guide.sh` on `SessionStart` (every source, compaction included) prints `sminos guide`'s text on a machine enrolled at a desk hub (`daemon/hub.json`, or `daemon/identity.json` once cut over to its endpoint): the session's alias, short, project and machine, where that project comes from (its launch directory, its repository's remote, the machine's own project, or its `join`), and the verbs that find and reach the org's other sessions, and after a compaction or a resume the roster of the members it exchanged sminos messages with, read from the logs (where each is now, its state and in-charge line, the tag that reaches it, its last exchange, and whether that is unanswered); nothing on an unenrolled machine or in an internal fork, and never an error (design §9 of the org directory spec).
