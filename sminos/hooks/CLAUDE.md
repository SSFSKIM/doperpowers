# sminos/hooks/ — the sminos plugin's hooks

A path here is named from `sminos/` when it is the plugin's own file; `docs/`, `sminos/…` and `to-human/…` paths are the repository's. The function hooks' detail is in `hooks/mods/CLAUDE.md`.

The shell hooks: `hooks/smns-record.sh` on `Stop` and `SessionStart` passes the payload to `sminos record`, which records the session's config dir when its engine runs under a non-default one (the hook alone runs in the session's environment: `skills/sminos/scripts/CLAUDE.md`), joins the session to its project and exports what it sent its human since the last export (its `<message>` spans, code-aware, its accepted calls of a message tool — `mcp__to-human__message` or MAWS's `mcp__maws__message` (`MESSAGE_TOOLS`), each a `message` event at the call's place whose `src` is the tool-use id, an unanswered call holding the export's position — and its async asks and their answers) into the project's log, an answer to a question another session of the machine asked going under that session's writer (a copy answering its original's), and none of what a copy copied; it is bounded (5 s, `SMNS_RECORD_TIMEOUT`), exits 0 on any failure, and does nothing outside a project or in sminos's own internal forks (`SMNS_INTERNAL`). A session with a question sheet (`$SMINOS_HOME/sheet/<session>/state.json`) passes the hook's project preflight wherever it is, which assigns it no project: at Stop `record` writes what its record owes the project log, each question's events to the project its question was first written in, and an event with no destination yet (a question owed while the session is in no project) waits in the record (`sminos sheet log`; `skills/sminos/scripts/CLAUDE.md`).

`hooks/smns-derive.sh` on `SessionStart` with the `compact` source starts `sminos derive <session> --compacted` detached (`nohup`, its output appended to `daemon.log` as the daemon's `derive --all` is, since a failed fork's reason is said only on stderr) and exits 0 at once, printing nothing, so every compaction derives the session's in-charge line, its summary and its charge name again rather than leaving them to the daemon's six-hour spacing; `--compacted` waives that spacing and the no-activity rule (a set line, a brief's or an operator's, never refuses any derive: it stands until the next derived line), and clears the session's `now:` status line (`derive_refusal`, `clear_status`; design `docs/doperpowers/specs/2026-11-08-sminos-derive-on-compact-design.md`, its every-third-compaction count replaced by §2 of `docs/doperpowers/specs/2026-11-23-sminos-session-search-design.md`). It runs in the session's environment, as a Bash tool's `sminos derive` does; nothing in an internal fork or for a payload whose `session_id` is not a UUID.

`hooks/smns-cleared.sh` on `SessionEnd` keeps the payload of a `/clear` (reason `clear`: the session the process is leaving) as `$SMINOS_HOME/cleared/<CLAUDE_PID>.json`, by one write and a rename and with no Python, since an ending session's hooks are given little time; nothing for another reason or in an internal fork, and never an error. The new session's record hook and guide hook, in the same process, read it as the witness of the session left (`cleared_from`: a start of source `clear`, a note younger than a minute, written after the process started, naming another session), and the record hook moves the alias to the new session (T1's M3); the daemon's sweep removes a note once its process is gone. Beside the note, written first, `<CLAUDE_PID>.sheet` holds the identity of the question sheet the session left (its record directory's device and inode and the nonce in its `.incarnation`, minted here if none is), so the daemon's sweep may remove that record and never one made after it: the witness permits the removal, it does not guarantee one (the sweep still checks, under the record's lock, that neither the record nor the transcript changed since the note).

`hooks/smns-guide.sh` on `SessionStart` (every source, compaction included) prints `sminos guide`'s text on a machine enrolled at a desk hub (`daemon/identity.json`, written at its endpoint's first welcome): the session's alias, short, project and machine, where that project comes from (its launch directory, its repository's remote, the machine's own project, or its `join`), that it is a thread (a lane A member; not said to a session MAWS launched), a line naming the channels its thread is in when it is in any (`sminos_channels.channels_of`; with where a reply line sends an answer: a member's frame's into the channel, a frame from a session not in it to that session's own project), and the verbs that find and reach the org's other sessions, speaking in a channel and, for a thread alone (`channel create` refuses a caller that is none), making one among them (CH2's M10, evaluated: without the channel line a session told to keep sessions of three repositories in one place asked them to `join` its project), and after a compaction or a resume the roster of the members it exchanged sminos messages with, read from the logs (where each is now, its state and in-charge line, the tag that reaches it, its last exchange, and whether that is unanswered); nothing on an unenrolled machine or in an internal fork, and never an error (design §9 of the org directory spec).

`hooks/smns-tasks.sh` on `SessionStart` (every source) is the Task Board's start line (`docs/doperpowers/plans/2026-11-27-task-board-b1.md`, M1): a hook of its own, not behind the guide's enrollment test, which starts no Python where `$SMINOS_HOME/tasks/state.json` does not exist or in an internal fork. It reads the payload's `session_id` and `source` without Python, runs `sminos task remind`, prints all but its first line (the keys), which the engine adds to the session's context, and only then runs `sminos task shown --keys` with exactly those keys (and with none, which still binds a `/clear`'s pair to the session's thread). Printing first and marking second: a failure between them, or of the mark alone, names the same acts at the next start; it claims the line was put in the context, never that it was read. A `remind` that fails prints nothing and marks nothing; every path exits 0. Its 30 s timeout holds the longest start, a `/clear` behind a held store lock: the pair's full 10 s, the bind's 3 s, the mark's 10 s.
