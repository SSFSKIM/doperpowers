The patch can serve expired values indefinitely, hang all retries after a factory failure, and serialize unrelated keyed operations. Its timeout, hit-rate, warm-up, and backoff-cap behavior also does not match the newly documented contracts.

For example, an entry with two fresh hits under the default threshold is served indefinitely when its third read occurs after expiry, contrary to the documented no-stale guarantee.

- [P1] Release the guard before awaiting a keyed lock — /private/var/folders/c1/l4z5k02n2779byvnymzxsvth0000gn/T/review-bench.ZfS6Y6/keylock.py:56-57
  The yielded caller block remains inside `async with self._guard`, so holding any key prevents callers from acquiring every other key. This serializes unrelated cache computations, makes nested keyed acquisitions deadlock, and prevents same-key timeout accounting from starting until the current holder exits. The guard should protect only the maps and reference counts, not lock acquisition or the caller's block.
